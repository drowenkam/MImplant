Use the website offline

After publishing to GitHub Pages, open your site in Safari while online, tap Share → Add to Home Screen, and launch it once online. All 800 SBA questions, searchable notes and oral-case text are saved by the website cache. The 82 formatted notebook pages can also be saved for offline viewing. PDFs are optional, downloaded separately to avoid storage failures.
